End a session you created, and everything under it.

Use this when you finish with a peer, whether it answered you or its work no longer matters. A session is a real operating-system process that holds a whole runtime. A peer that reported back and waits for nothing costs what it cost while it worked. Four peers you finished with an hour ago are four idle processes.

Nothing ends a session on its own. The harness reaps yours when you end, so a short turn cleans up for you. A long turn does not, and finished peers accumulate while it runs.

Keep a peer only while you might message it again. A follow-up — a correction, a narrowing, one more question — needs the session alive and holding its context. That is worth keeping where you truly expect to follow up. Where you do not, end it.
