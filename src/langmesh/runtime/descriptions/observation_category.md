What kind of finding this is.

- `fact` — something established about the code, the system or the world.
- `decision` — a choice committed to, and why it beat the alternative.
- `constraint` — a limit the work ran into that rules an approach out: what the environment, the tooling or the data forbids. A rule the person stated is not this; it belongs to their own record.
- `failure` — an approach that did not work, and how it failed.
- `artifact` — a file, path, command or resource that matters, by its exact name.
- `open` — work unfinished or blocked, with the next concrete step it implies. When it is answered later, supersede it with what is now the case, as a `retraction`.
