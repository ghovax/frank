What kind of instruction this is.

- `requirement` — what they asked to be built, changed or found out.
- `correction` — where they redirected the work, and away from what. Record what was being done when they stopped it, since that is what makes it a correction rather than a preference.
- `preference` — how they want the work done: naming, structure, tone, tools, process. These govern work they have not seen yet, and they are the easiest to lose.
