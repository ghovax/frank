# Web research

Use this skill when a task needs up-to-date or external information.

1. **Search broadly first.** Issue several `web_search` queries covering different angles and phrasings. Don't stop at the first result.
2. **Read primary sources.** Open the actual pages (via `bash` with `curl`/`wget` to stdout) rather than relying only on search snippets. Prefer official docs, original announcements, and primary data over aggregators.
3. **Cross-check.** Confirm important claims against at least two independent sources. Note disagreements explicitly.
4. **Cite.** For every non-obvious claim, include the source URL. Distinguish facts from your inference.
5. **Report.** End with a self-contained summary: the key findings, the evidence, and any open questions — not a pile of links.
